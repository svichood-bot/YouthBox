How YouthBox works 🎶

  1. Tap a picture to play that song.
  2. The smiley button plays and pauses.
  3. Swipe the bottom bar up (or tap the ☰) to see what’s coming next. Swipe it down to close.
  4. The arrows jump to the next or last song.
  5. Shuffle mixes up the order. Tap it again to turn it off.
  6. Use 🔍 Find a song to look for a song. Your songs show first. Under the dashed line are more songs you can ask for. Tap 🙋 Ask and a grown-up will say yes or no.
  7. Tap 🎤 Artists or 💿 Albums to find songs that way.
  8. Swipe the bottom bar up and tap 🎤 Lyrics to sing along.
  9. Bouncy pink bars show which song is playing.
  10. Tap the 🤍 on a song to keep it in ❤️ Favourites.
  11. Tap ➕ New list to make your own playlist from the songs here.
  12. Tap the ＋ on a song to add it to the 🎧 Queue or to one of your lists.
  13. If a song can’t play, YouthBox hides it and skips to the next one.
  14. When it says Music break, it’s time to do something else. See you tomorrow!
For grown-ups

Tap Grown-ups (you’ll need your PIN), paste a YouTube or YouTube Music link for a song or a playlist, then tap Add. A playlist link adds all of its songs.

Spotify links don’t work here. To use a Spotify playlist, move it to YouTube Music with a transfer tool such as TuneMyMusic or Soundiiz (free plans may limit how many songs), then paste the new link.

You can save your list from the Grown-ups panel to move it to Spotify or YouTube Music later.

For grown-ups: stop the ads (YouTube Premium sign-in)

YouthBox plays songs through YouTube’s own player. That player only skips ads for a YouTube Premium or YouTube Music Premium account that is signed in in the same browser that is showing YouthBox. It can’t be signed in from inside YouthBox, and it has nothing to do with the YouTube Kids app.

  1. On the kids’ device, open the browser they use (Safari on iPhone or iPad, Chrome on Android).
  2. Go to youtube.com, tap your profile icon or Sign in, and use the Google account that has Premium.
  3. Check it worked: your profile picture shows and normal YouTube videos play without ads.
  4. Open YouthBox in that same browser (not a private or incognito tab) and play a song.

Still seeing ads?

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings → Safari → turn off Prevent Cross-Site Tracking.
  • Chrome: allow third-party cookies for youtube.com.
  • YouthBox on the Home Screen: it keeps its own storage and is usually not signed in. Open YouthBox in the browser itself instead.

Good to know: this signs the whole browser in to your account, so anyone using it can reach your YouTube account. Use a device you control, or a separate browser profile just for YouthBox. On a Premium family plan you can sign in with a family member’s account instead of your own. This can’t be guaranteed on every device, and ads with a countdown can’t be skipped until it ends.

Skip button: swipe the bottom bar up to open the player. The pink picture covers the video (and any ad). If you need YouTube’s Skip button, open Grown-ups and turn on the Skip ad window, or turn off Hide the video.

        YouTube key: turns on song search, playlist import and song checks

        This is the most reliable way to read playlists. It takes about five minutes, once. The same key lets the kids search for more songs to ask for.

        1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and create a project.
        2. Open APIs & Services → Library, search for YouTube Data API v3 and click Enable.
        3. Open APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → API key.
        4. Edit the key. Under Application restrictions pick Websites and add your YouthBox address ending in /* (for example https://yourname.github.io/*). Under API restrictions pick YouTube Data API v3. Save.
        5. Paste the key below and tap Save key. It stays in this browser only.

        Also remove rejected songs from the YouTube playlist (optional)

        YouTube only lets an app change a playlist when you sign in. Once: in the same Google Cloud project open APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID, pick Web application, add your YouthBox address under Authorised JavaScript origins, and add your Google account as a test user on the consent screen.             In the library

              Songs that can’t play are hidden from the kids automatically. Checking and finding replacements needs the YouTube key.

              How song checking works
              1. Songs stay hidden from the kids until they pass every check. This covers new songs from a playlist refresh, a pasted link, or a song you approve. In the library they show as “being checked”.
              2. The checks: it can play inside apps, it isn’t age-restricted or marked explicit, it isn’t blocked in your country, and the title and lyrics have no swearing, sexual, violent or blasphemy words. The first three need the YouTube key.
              3. Lyrics are looked up a few songs at a time while the app is open, so a big playlist can take a while to appear.
              4. A song that fails goes to Held back (tap Allow if you’re happy with it) or is removed if it can’t play.
              5. Songs you approve from the kids’ requests skip the word and explicit checks, because you decided. They still need to be able to play in apps.
              6. Rarely a song only fails when played. That one is removed the first time it errors.
